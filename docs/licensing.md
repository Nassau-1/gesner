## What "free forever" means here
Official releases have no price, subscription, paid tier or telemetry business
model. Existing compliant recipients retain their granted rights for the
version they received, and redistribution must preserve free-of-charge access
and the same terms.

A license cannot guarantee that nobody will violate it, that maintenance will
continue forever, or that future independently owned code will use identical
terms. Copyright holders still own their respective contributions. No promise
of permanent hosting or support is made.

## Source-available, not OSI open source
The [Open Source Definition](https://opensource.org/osd) permits commercial use
and redistribution. Because this license prohibits monetizing the software, we
describe it as source-available rather than OSI-approved open source.

A blanket [PolyForm Noncommercial license](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0)
was not chosen: the project explicitly permits free business use and requires
source-sharing terms for redistributed versions.

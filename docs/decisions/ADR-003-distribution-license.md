# ADR-003: Free use and source-sharing distribution
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-08

## Context
The maintainer wants free personal and business use, community contributions,
and a prohibition on monetizing the software. Distributed modifications should
retain source access and the same terms.

## Decision
Use a project-specific Free Use and Share-Alike license. Describe the software
as source-available, not OSI-approved open source. Keep existing compliant
grants irrevocable and require free distribution with exact-version source.

## Alternatives
An OSI open-source license would permit sales. Blanket noncommercial licenses
would restrict the free business use that is expressly intended. Neither
matches the requested combination.

## Consequences
Custom licensing can reduce adoption and compatibility with other projects.
The terms have not been independently reviewed by counsel. Future contributions
remain owned by their contributors and enter under the same project license.
